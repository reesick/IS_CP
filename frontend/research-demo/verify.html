      How the proof works

      1. Fingerprint. Every original, protected export and AI edit from the pipeline is registered with its SHA-256 and a 256-bit perceptual hash.
      2. Protection pattern. For copies that are not byte-identical, both registered versions are put through the same resizing and JPEG steps, and the closest one wins, so a protected copy is told apart from the clean original.
      3. Tamper-evident log. Every registration and check is appended to a SHA-256 hash chain. Editing any past entry breaks the chain.
