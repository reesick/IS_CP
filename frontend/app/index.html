About this export
The image is fit inside a 512 × 512 research canvas, preserving its proportions. Padding is excluded from editing. Transparent pixels are composited on a neutral background. Both sides of a paired edit test use this exact canvas. Protection applies to the selected mask and tested editor; other regions and editors require new evaluation.
